ECHO for Windows 1.0: your Navidrome library and ECHO's Listen room under liquid glass, made for a gaming PC with ECHO on a second monitor.

**Everything in it:** Home, Your Library (albums, artists, playlists, Liked Songs, Wanted), search, the queue sheet, shuffle and repeat; the Listen room with word-by-word lyrics over a room in the album's colours that moves with the music; full screen on any monitor (F11), and it comes back there next time; media keys and Windows' media overlay while you play; settings, your sessions and devices; the keyboard shortcuts (press **?**). Your queue follows you between here, the web and your phone.

**Glass:** it darkens over bright covers so text stays readable, and turns opaque if you switch off Windows' Transparency effects.

**Not in this app yet:** YouTube Music mode, and the admin pages and Navidrome connection (use the web for those).

**Windows may say it protected your PC** (the exe is not signed): More info → Run anyway.
