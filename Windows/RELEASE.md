ECHO for Windows, now in Cider's look: the window, sidebar, player bar, pages, queue and lyrics of Cider 1, without the Apple Music parts.

- **Two layouts**, as Cider: Mojave (player at the bottom, the default) and Maverick (player in the top bar), in Settings → Visual.
- **Home** with Recently Played, Your Favorites and Made For You; **Recently Added, Songs, Albums, Artists** and your playlists in the sidebar; album, playlist, mix and artist pages; search from the top bar.
- **Queue and lyrics** in Cider's side panel (lyrics light up word by word); the **fullscreen view** and the **mini player** from the cover in the player bar.
- Your queue carries over from the web and phone, as before.

**Windows may say it protected your PC** (the exe is not signed): More info → Run anyway.
