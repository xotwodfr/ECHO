ECHO for Windows, an early build: liquid glass, your albums, and the room.

**Try:** sign in (two-factor works), open an album, play a song, then click the cover in the glass
player to open the room, which moves with the music.

**Not yet:** lyrics, the queue, search, playlists, settings, media keys. They come in the next
builds.

**Windows may say it protected your PC** (the exe is not signed): More info → Run anyway.
