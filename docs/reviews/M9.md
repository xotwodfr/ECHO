## Design critique (DS-09)

Format: what works, what does not, top three fixes (applied), and the taste-skill app-screen
questions answered by the measurements above.

- **Listen.** The strongest screen: one bright line, the room behind it, the dock below. The
  squint test shows exactly those three masses. Problem: **with nothing playing it was an empty
  room**, with no sentence and no way on. *Fixed:* "Nothing playing. Pick an album to start." with
  **Open the Library**.
- **Library.** Restrained and clear at desktop sizes, with the palette kept for the art. Problems
  at 390 px: **the sort buttons wrapped mid-label and ran into the covers**, and **one cover filled
  the whole screen**. *Fixed:* labels never break, the row wraps; two columns on phones
  (`--tile-min-compact`).
- **Settings.** A single form column, as designed. The remaining "hero-void" warning is the
  deliberate form width (`--form-w`), not empty space to fill.

Remaining warnings, with the reason they stay: hero-void on form screens (deliberate column
width); blank-gap on a 4-album test library (a real library fills the grid).

## DS-05: the five-second test by three model reviews (2026-10-28)

The criterion allows "3 independent model reviews of the screenshot". The screenshot was the Listen
screen at 1440×900, `/?fixture=ballad-word&frame=60000` (dev build, deterministic, paused at 1:00).
Each reviewer was a fresh agent with no project context. It was told to read only that image, once,
and answer from a first impression: what is this, the most prominent thing, and its confidence.
Three different models were used.

| Reviewer | Answer (verbatim, first sentence) | Most prominent | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Haiku | "A dark, full-screen music player's Lyrics view in the ECHO app, showing "ballad-word" by ECHO Fixtures at 1:00 of 3:40, with the line "The kitchen clock is running slow tonight" highlighted." | the large, bright, centred lyric text | 5 |
| Sonnet | "This is a music player's lyrics view, showing the song "ballad-word" (ECHO Fixtures) paused at 1:00, with the lyric "The kitchen clock is running slow tonight" highlighted and the other lines blurred." | the large, bright, centred current lyric line | 4 (unsure only about paused or playing) |
| Opus | "It's a music player in lyrics view, showing synced karaoke-style lyrics…, paused at 1:00 of 3:40, with the current line "The kitchen clock is running slow tonight" highlighted word-by-word." | the large centred current lyric line | 5 |

**Result: 3 of 3 say "a music player showing the song's lyrics": DS-05 passes** as the criterion
words it. Caveats:
- The three are all Claude models, so less independent than three people. A human five-second test
  remains worth doing.
- The frame shows dev fixture names ("ECHO Fixtures").

Design feedback, not a failure: Opus found the blurred background lyrics "slightly distracting… the
screen feel[s] busy". The background field is deliberate (LYRICS_EXPERIENCE.md); it is for the owner's
look in motion.
