### Decisions taken (routine)

- **The quantiser is seeded.** Its k-means seeds the first assignment from `Math.random()`, so the
  same cover gave slightly different palettes on recompute (accent L 0.7081 vs 0.7051, found by the
  cache-version test). §6.1 promises every client the identical palette; `clustersOf` swaps in a
  fixed LCG for the synchronous call.
- **`Score.score` is not used.** §6.2 step 3 names it, but every role is chosen by its own explicit
  rule from the population map; Score's ranking would decide nothing.
- **material-color-utilities is patched** (`patches/`) to export `./quantize/*`: its index imports
  modules with extensionless ESM specifiers that Node cannot load. The quantiser's own files are
  clean. The Docker image copies `patches/` before install and `src/shared` into the runtime stage.
- **`inkActive`** = L 0.95, the highlight's hue, C = min(0.012, 0.15 × highlight C) (gives the
  fallback token exactly).
- **The palette lives at shell level, not in the Stage engine** (which loads lazily with three.js);
  the engine follows `paletteStore`.
- **The unsung-ink mix (M3 design review) applies at the anchor only**, fading in as a line arrives
  (`unlitMixAt`). Applied at every depth it pushed +2 under 3:1 even on the darkest room.
- **Contrast is measured per band.** The active line is checked against the probe rows behind it,
  the upcoming lines against theirs (§3.4 rule 1: the worst case behind each role).

### Open decision for the owner: +3 cannot reach 3:1

§3.4 rule 4 asks +1…+3 to reach 3:1. With §4.1's depth table, +3 (z 0.66) is drawn at opacity 0.40
with its ink 36% toward the room: **≈ 3.3:1 on the darkest room the palette allows (L 0.12), ≈ 2.7:1
on the room as rendered** (pools and key light lift the corridor's peak to L ≈ 0.26–0.30 even after
−40% exposure; measured on all 30 fixture palettes). Enforcing it would pin the lyric zone at −40% on
every song. Until decided, the solver enforces active, lit, +1 and +2 and reports +3
(`ENFORCED_ZONE` in `contrast.ts`). Options: raise +3's opacity (≈ 0.55 at z 0.66 would pass on the
rendered room), drop the depth ink-mix for +3, or set +3's floor to the decorative tier.

### Finding: small bright subjects do not make the accent

Harbour Lights (navy with small gold lights) gets a pale-blue accent: the gold is 0.2% of a 128 px
cover, and §6.2's `population^0.5 · C` picks the navy. Per spec; real covers usually have larger
subjects. A taste question for M6's real-library pass.
