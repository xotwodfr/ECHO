### Security review findings fixed along the way

The automated review of each commit found five issues; all fixed with regression tests that fail on
the old code: second-factor guesses not counted across challenges and recovery codes spendable twice
concurrently (`a543a53`); IPv6 forms hiding a private IPv4, untrusted content types from the proxied
server, unbounded JSON buffering (`400081f`, `8a2b034`); and the palette cache shared across
Navidrome servers (`53ce42c`).

### Decisions taken (routine)

- **Mutating Subsonic methods are POST at ECHO's proxy** (`star`, `unstar`, `scrobble`), forwarded
  as GET: with a `SameSite=Lax` cookie, a GET would let another site star or scrobble for a user.
- **`GET /session`** was added to the unauthenticated surface (§5.5 updated): the client asks who it
  is before rendering, and a 401 on `/me` logged a console error on every anonymous page.
- **Development builds keep the fixture shell without a session;** production builds send visitors
  to `/login` or `/setup`. The provider is chosen at start, so signing in or connecting reloads.
- **`ECHO_DEV_FIXTURES=1`** (E2E only) lets the palette route answer for generated covers without a
  session; production requires one.
- **The E2E base URL is `localhost`**: Firefox keeps a `Secure` (`__Host-`) cookie over http only
  there.
- **Reverb's limiter lets ring and flare light fall freely** (the feedback buffer softens falls);
  rises and exposure share the 0.24/s budget. Rendered frames confirm it (A11Y-08 below).
- **Bloom and halation are a small blur of the energy buffer** in the composite, not the
  `postprocessing` chain. Revisit with the governor (M9) if the look needs more.
