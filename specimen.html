Long lines at real measures

Each box is the real lyric measure (0.64 × width) at the real --ly-active size for that viewport. Rule (§4.3): two balanced rows. If a third row is needed, Mona Sans condenses first (to wdth 82), then either face shrinks by up to 12%, and only then are three rows allowed.
